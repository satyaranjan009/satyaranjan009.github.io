About

I am a Marie Curie Doctoral Fellow at the Fritz Haber Research Centre for Molecular Dynamics, Hebrew University of Jerusalem, working under Prof. Noam Agmon. My research lies at the intersection of computational biophysics, quantum chemistry, and machine learning — with a particular focus on proton and ion transport mechanisms in hydrated biological macromolecules.

Using classical Molecular Dynamics and hybrid QM/MM simulations, I investigate proton transfer along protein surfaces and collagen-like peptides, aiming to elucidate the biophysical basis of processes fundamental to cell bioenergetics, enzyme catalysis, and disease mechanisms.
